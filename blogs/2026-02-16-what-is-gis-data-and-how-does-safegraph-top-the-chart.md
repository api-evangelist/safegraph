Key Takeaways GIS data combines geographic coordinates with descriptive attributes to model the real world digitally. Reliable GIS analysis depends on accuracy, freshness, consistent schemas, and broad coverage. Many organizations struggle with outdated or incomplete GIS datasets that require heavy cleaning.
