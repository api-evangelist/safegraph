Key Takeaways Spatial autocorrelation measures the degree to which values at one location are related to values at nearby locations. Positive spatial autocorrelation means similar values concentrate together geographically; negative means dissimilar values appear adjacent. Ignoring spatial dependency in analysis can lead to flawed conclusions and poor predictive models.
